ProCapture — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

ProCapture is a screen recorder that runs entirely on your own computer. This policy explains the only situation in which any data leaves your device.

What ProCapture does not do

  • It does not create an account, and does not ask for your name or email to work.
  • It does not upload, stream or back up your recordings, screenshots or audio. Everything you capture stays in the folder you choose (by default Videos\ProCapture) until you move or delete it.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no usage or telemetry tracking. Nothing is sent when you record, edit or export.

The one thing that is sent — and only if you choose to send it

If you use Report a problem, the report you submit is sent to the developer so the issue can be investigated. Before sending, the app shows you the exact contents via See exactly what will be sent. A report contains:

  • the description you type, and your email address only if you enter one;
  • the app version, Windows version, CPU/RAM summary, screen resolutions and locale;
  • your ProCapture settings (quality, format, which sources are enabled) — this excludes anything you typed, such as watermark text or your save-folder path;
  • the last portion of the app's diagnostic log, which records app events and errors. Your Windows account name is removed from all paths before sending.

You can untick Include technical details to send only your description. Reports never contain your recordings, screenshots, audio, keystrokes or the contents of your screen.

Reports are delivered by email through the form service FormSubmit and are used solely to diagnose and fix the problem you reported. They are not sold, shared or used for marketing. If you provided an email address, it is used only to reply to you.

Purchases

If you buy ProCapture through the Microsoft Store, the purchase is handled entirely by Microsoft under its own terms and privacy policy. ProCapture does not see, receive or store your payment details.

Data stored on your computer

  • Recordings and screenshots — in your chosen output folder.
  • Settings — in %APPDATA%\ProCapture.
  • A diagnostic log — in %APPDATA%\ProCapture\logs, capped at about 2 MB and overwritten over time. Open it any time with Settings → Open log folder.
  • Undelivered reports — if a report cannot be sent (for example, you are offline), it is kept in %APPDATA%\ProCapture\reports and sent on a later launch. Deleting the file cancels it.

None of this is transmitted anywhere except as described above.

Permissions ProCapture uses

  • Screen capture — to record your screen or a window, only while recording.
  • Microphone and camera — only when you switch those sources on. Windows shows its own indicator while they are in use.
  • Keyboard and mouse monitoring — used only for the optional on-screen click, cursor-highlight and keystroke-display effects. These are off by default, they only draw shapes on screen while a recording is running, and nothing about your input is stored or transmitted.

Children

ProCapture is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the app and the date above will change.

Contact

Use Report a problem inside ProCapture, and include your email address if you would like a reply.

You can also reach the developer through the support form.
